Agents Pocket

Your coding agents, in your pocket.

Read, answer and approve what Claude, Codex and OpenCode ask on your own computers, from wherever you are.

Download on theApp Store

Three facts

  1. 01

    Your computers, your keys.

    Agents keep running where they already run. The app reaches them over SSH, with keys that stay on your phone.

  2. 02

    Every agent, one list.

    Claude, Codex, OpenCode and the rest show up as sessions you can read from start to finish.

  3. 03

    Approve in a tap.

    When an agent asks for permission or a decision, you see the question, answer it, and it carries on.

How it works

  1. Run your agents in herdr on your Mac or Linux machine.
  2. Install the Agents Pocket plugin and show a pairing code.
  3. Scan it with the app. Your Host shows up with its agents.
